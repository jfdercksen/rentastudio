Terms & Conditions

Please read the following carefully before confirming your booking.

These Terms & Conditions govern the use of the studio at Kyalami Estates, Johannesburg ("the Studio"). By booking and paying for a session, you confirm that you have read, understood and agreed to these terms.

01 Booking & Payment

  • Confirmation: Bookings are only confirmed once full payment and the breakage deposit have been received and reflected.
  • Payment methods: Payment is processed securely via PayFast — card, EFT, SnapScan and Zapper are accepted.
  • Quoted prices: All prices listed are in South African Rand (ZAR) and include VAT where applicable.
  • Weekend / after-hours: Weekend rates apply Friday to Sunday. A surcharge may apply to bookings outside standard operating hours — we will confirm this with you before your slot is locked in.

02 Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • More than 48 hours before: Free reschedule to any available future slot.
  • Within 48 hours: A 50% cancellation fee applies. The remaining 50% may be credited towards a future booking within 60 days.
  • No-shows: Non-refundable. The full amount is forfeited.
  • Force majeure: In the rare case we need to cancel on our side (power, weather, emergency), you will receive a full refund or free reschedule.

03 Breakage / Damage Deposit

  • A refundable deposit of R750 is required with every booking.
  • The deposit covers: damage to studio equipment (lighting, stands, backdrops, cameras), missing or broken items, excessive cleaning, and any overtime beyond the booked hours.
  • Clients are responsible for any damage exceeding the deposit amount. Any deductions will be communicated with photographic proof.
  • Refunds: Processed within 48–72 business hours after your session, once the studio has been inspected. Weekends and public holidays are excluded from this window.
Important — Equipment use: All equipment (camera, lenses, lighting, microphones, teleprompter, green & black screen, chroma keyer, livestream kit) is provided exclusively for use within the studio during your booked session. It is not available for external hire or removal from the premises.

04 Identification & Access

  • A valid ID or Passport is required at the time of booking and must be presented on arrival at the studio.
  • Uploaded ID documents are stored securely and used only for booking confirmation and access verification.
  • Access is granted only during your booked hours. Early arrival or late departure may incur additional fees deducted from your deposit.

05 Studio Rules

  • No food, drinks or alcohol inside the studio space unless pre-approved. Complimentary coffee and water are provided in the designated area.
  • Children & minors: Must be supervised by a parent or guardian at all times. Studio staff are not permitted to hold, dress or directly supervise minors.
  • Equipment handling: Clients and guests must not touch, move or operate studio equipment without permission. Studio equipment is not a prop — please do not lean on lights, stands, backdrops or cameras.
  • Smoking: Strictly prohibited anywhere on the premises.
  • Respect the space: The studio is part of a private home within Kyalami Estates. Please keep noise reasonable and respect neighbouring residents.

06 Studio Availability

  • Bookings operate on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • We reserve the right to decline or cancel a booking that conflicts with our schedule, policies, or community rules — in which case a full refund will be issued.

  • Clients use the studio at their own risk. The Studio is not liable for any injury, loss or damage to persons or property incurred during a booking, except where caused by gross negligence on our part.
  • Clients using additional crew, subjects or models are responsible for ensuring those parties have agreed to abide by these terms.
